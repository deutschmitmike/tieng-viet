# Week 1, day 5: who you are, in Saigon Vietnamese

## What today is for

The first four days were sound. Today the sounds carry content: the self-introduction you will send to every tandem, plus the small set of Saigon contractions and particles that make it sound like Saigon rather than a textbook. Half of today's track is sentences you already know from days 1 to 4, now in the order of a real first conversation.

## The persona sentences

Anh là người Đức. Anh là giáo viên tiếng Đức. Anh dạy tiếng Đức cho người Trung Quốc. Ba anh là người Việt. Má anh là người Trung Quốc. Anh nói được tiếng Trung. Anh mới học tiếng Việt.

Seven sentences, and they answer the first seven questions every Vietnamese person asks. Learn them as one block; by the end of the day they should come out without thinking.

Note ba and má: that is how the South says dad and mom. Hanoi says bố and mẹ; you will read those, never say them.

## Saigon contractions

anh ấy becomes ảnh, cô ấy becomes cổ, ông ấy becomes ổng, bà ấy becomes bả, chị ấy becomes chỉ. The tone of the second word (ấy, sắc) folds into the first as a hỏi tone. Everyone in Saigon says these; the full forms sound like reading aloud.

## Particles

- hả at the end turns a statement into a checking question: Em ở Sài Gòn hả? Like 是喔 or 嗎 with a raised eyebrow.
- nha softens a request or a statement: Em nói chậm chút nha. Mai nói tiếp nha. Close to 喔 or 吧.
- nghen is the warmer, more familiar nha: Ngủ ngon nghen.
- Thiệt hả? and Vậy hả? are the two reactions you will hear most in a chat. Thiệt is the Southern form of thật.

## Mandarin bridge

Anh là người Đức = 我是德國人, word for word, including the topic-first shape. Anh nói được tiếng Trung: được after the verb is 得 in 說得 / 會, "manage to". Người Trung Quốc: 中國 read in Hán Việt is Trung Quốc; you now own your first two 漢字 correspondences, 中 = trung and 國 = quốc.

## Today's output block

Send the seven persona sentences to one tandem as a message, in Vietnamese, with Em ở Sài Gòn hả? at the end. Ask them to correct anything. Their answer is your first piece of real Saigon input; save it, we use it in week 2.

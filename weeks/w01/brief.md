# Week 1 brief (21 to 25 September 2026)

## This week

The Saigon sound system in four days, your self-introduction on the fifth. 121 sentences and drill rows, about 25 per day. Everything you say this week is in the persona: German teacher in Germany, Chinese students, Vietnamese father, Chinese mother.

- d1 tones (five in Saigon), greetings, anh / em
- d2 vowels, length, glides; coffee and weather phrases
- d3 initials with their Saigon values; understanding and slowing people down
- d4 finals, the Saigon mergers, numbers; expensive, learning, not understanding
- d5 persona self-introduction, contractions ảnh / cổ / ổng, particles hả / nha / nghen

## Before Monday: the voice test

All audio for week 1 is already generated and assembled. Nothing to run. Press PLAY on these six and listen for one thing each:

@@play s0043 s0049 s0051 s0014 s0061 s0077

- s0043 (dạ, dài, dễ, gì, giờ, giá): all six must start with a y sound. If they start with z, the voice is Northern for d/gi.
- s0049 (rồi, ra, rất, rẻ, rửa): must be an r, not z.
- s0051 (trà, chà): the two must sound different.
- s0014 (khỏe, hỏi, ở, cũng, nữa): cũng and nữa should have the same smooth dip-rise as the first three; a break in the middle means Northern ngã.
- s0061 (quá, quê, quen, quán): should start like w.
- s0077 (tin, tinh): if the two are audibly different, the voice is not doing the Saigon merger.

If two or more of these fail, tell Claude which ones. Options, in order: another Vietnamese voice from the ElevenLabs library (you pick the voice, Claude swaps the ID and regenerates), or keep the voice and let Claude respell the worst cases in the tts column so the voice says them the Saigon way. Also say if the speed feels too fast for shadowing; week 1 can be regenerated at 0.9.
